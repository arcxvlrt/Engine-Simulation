## 1. AngeTheGreat (Ange Yaghi) Engine Simulator: GitHub, YouTube, Patreon, Discord, Steam

### Takeaway
The open-source engine-sim is a mid-sized niche hit: about 9.6k GitHub stars and about 950 forks, a Discord of about 25.5k members, a Patreon of about 7.6k members, and a YouTube channel of about 222k subscribers (all from snippets, undated). The commercial combustion "Engine Simulator" on Steam (app 2381500) was still unreleased at the last indexed snapshot, but the same developer's spin-off "Steam Engine Simulator" (app 2381620, released June 2023) has about 7k English and about 9k total reviews at 98% positive. That makes it the strongest proof that this audience will pay.

### Inferences
- About 9.6k stars after roughly 4 years, with older caches at about 9.2k, means GitHub growth has slowed to a trickle since the 2022 spike. The "project has moved" banner suggests development shifted to the closed commercial version and/or the community edition.
- If the Patreon "7,568 total members" figure is current, it is a sizeable paying-or-following core for a single-developer niche tool. Combined with the 25k Discord, the hardcore community is roughly 10k–30k people. The casual audience (YouTube viewers) is likely an order of magnitude larger.
- Rough sales estimate for Steam Engine Simulator: applying the common "Boxleiter" review-to-sales heuristic (roughly 30–60 sales per review; the heuristic itself was not sourced in this research) to about 9k reviews gives roughly 270k–540k units. Treat this as order-of-magnitude only.
- The combustion "Engine Simulator" being unreleased on Steam (as of the latest snippets) leaves a gap: no polished, easy-to-access, real-time physical engine sound sim exists for casual users. Today they must download a Windows build from GitHub. A free browser version would target exactly that gap.

## 2. Other Steam titles in this space (Automation, Car Mechanic Simulator)

### Takeaway
Adjacent car-tinkering games have large but older audiences. Car Mechanic Simulator 2021 has about 38k reviews and an all-time peak of about 22.5k concurrent players. Automation (the closest "engine designer" game) has about 16k reviews and an all-time peak of only about 1.5k concurrent players. Neither synthesizes engine sound from physics.

### Cited Findings
- **Automation – The Car Company Tycoon Game** (Camshaft Software, app 293760): about 16k reviews, 91.41% positive (SteamDB snippet); Steambase shows 15,938 total reviews with a 94/100 score; SteamSpy's older figure is 93% — [SteamDB 293760](https://steamdb.info/app/293760/); [Steambase reviews](https://steambase.io/games/automation-the-car-company-tycoon-game/reviews/); [SteamSpy](https://steamspy.com/app/293760)
- Automation all-time peak: **1,494 concurrent players on 12/20/2024** (Steambase) vs **1,468** (Steam Charts). Recent 24-hour peaks were 619 (Steam Charts) to 778 (Sensor Tower), with live counts around 550–766 at snapshot time (snippets, undated) — [Steambase charts](https://steambase.io/games/automation-the-car-company-tycoon-game/steam-charts); [Steam Charts](https://steamcharts.com/app/293760); [Sensor Tower](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/game/automation-the-car-company-tycoon-game)
- Automation's three pillars are engine design, car design and a tycoon simulator. Cars can be exported to BeamNG.drive as drivable vehicles — [Wikipedia: Automation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automation_(video_game))
- **Car Mechanic Simulator 2021** (app 1190000): launch-week peak of 17,530 concurrent players at about 92% positive (GamePressure citing SteamDB, Aug 2021) — [GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/playways-car-mechanic-simulator-2021-successful/z73794)
- CMS 2021 all-time peak: about 22,500 (Tracker.gg) to 22,530 (live-player-count.com); sources conflict slightly — [Tracker.gg](https://tracker.gg/population/steam/1190000); [live-player-count](https://live-player-count.com/game/car-mechanic-simulator-2021)

## 6. Is interest growing, flat, or declining since the 2022 viral peak?

### Takeaway
The available evidence points to a long-tail plateau after the August 2022 peak, not growth. GitHub stars crept from about 9.2k to about 9.6k, the open-source repo is marked "moved", and the commercial combustion sim was still unreleased. The niche is still alive, though: a 25k Discord, a 7.6k-member Patreon, steady new Steam reviews for Steam Engine Simulator, and new 2025–2026 mobile apps imitating the concept. No measured trend data was available to confirm direction.

### Cited Findings
- GitHub went from 9.2k stars and 847 forks (older cache) to 9.6k stars and 945–954 forks (newer), with a "project has moved" banner (snippets, undated) — [GitHub repo](https://github.com/ange-yaghi/engine-sim); [Star History](https://www.star-history.com/ange-yaghi/engine-sim/)
- Combustion Engine Simulator on Steam had no release date and was asking for wishlists (snippet, undated) — [Steam store 2381500](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2381500/Engine_Simulator/)
- Steam Engine Simulator was still collecting reviews (35 in the last 30 days, 94% positive) in a recent undated snapshot — [Steam store 2381620](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2381620/Steam_Engine_Simulator/)
- Discord had 25,494 members and Patreon 7,568 total members (undated) — [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/engine-sim-official); [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/atg_engine_simulator/about)
- New iOS apps marketing synthesized engine sound (ids 6772016143, 6787443044) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/engine-simulator/id6772016143); [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/enjine-sim-engine-sound-sim/6787443044)

### Inferences
- The core enthusiast base (about 10k–30k) looks stable, and the broader casual audience is large but episodic. Spikes are driven by AngeTheGreat uploads and press, and an eventual Steam release of Engine Simulator would likely cause a new spike, which a free browser product could ride (or compete against).
- For an ad-funded browser product, the casual "engine sound toy" audience (millions of mobile installs) is probably the larger traffic pool. The hardcore engine-sim community is smaller but supplies content (engine files, videos) that drives discovery.

### Gaps
- No time-series data (Google Trends, YouTube view history, Discord growth, Steam follower history) to measure the trend quantitatively. Every trend statement above is inferred from point-in-time, undated snippets.
